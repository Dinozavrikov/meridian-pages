What it does

Laboratories

A shared space around one question. Invite people, give them roles, save snapshots, and see who predicts best.

Your thinking in one place

Observations, hypotheses, predictions, beliefs, questions and tasks — connected into a graph you can explore.

Honest-thinking tools

Ask “what would prove me wrong?”, check whether your evidence is varied enough, separate facts from interpretations, and see how well your forecasts match reality.

Local AI

Translation, classification, embeddings and image description run on a local model via Ollama. No external AI service is used.

Nothing gets lost

Every edit has a history with undo, deleted things wait in a trash you can restore from, and you can attach files.

Privacy

Private messages are encrypted in the database, and backups are encrypted and regularly test-restored.

Stack

Frontend Next.js, React, TypeScript
Backend FastAPI (Python 3.12), SQLAlchemy, Alembic
Data PostgreSQL with pgvector, Redis
AI Ollama (local models)
Infra Docker Compose
